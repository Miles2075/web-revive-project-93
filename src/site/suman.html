About Suman Industries

Founded in 2017 in Vadodara, Gujarat, Suman Industries is dedicated to providing specialized engineering, construction, and installation services for the oil and gas industry. With a focus on delivering customized solutions, we enhance operational efficiency through innovative and reliable results.

Our skilled team excels in all aspects of fabrication, equipment installation, and pipework maintenance, ensuring that each project meets the highest standards of safety and efficiency. We are proud to be ISO 9001:2015 certified, underscoring our commitment to quality and continuous improvement. This certification reflects our dedication to consistently exceeding client expectations and establishing ourselves as a trusted industry partner.

As we continue to grow, Suman Industries remains committed to building lasting relationships with our clients and business partners. Our unwavering focus on integrity, innovation, and excellence drives us to evolve and adapt in a dynamic sector. We look forward to delivering value through every project, helping shape the future of the oil and gas industry with dependable and cutting-edge solutions.

Our mission is to exceed customer expectations by providing high-quality products and reliable service, fostering long-term partnerships built on honesty and integrity. We focus on delivering consistent value to meet the evolving needs of our clients.

Our Services

We provide customized gas pipeline design and applications tailored to the specific needs of the gas industry. Our services adhere to internationally recognized standards based on client preferences. Additionally, we offer comprehensive fabrication, electrical, and instrumentation services as part of our turnkey packages, utilizing qualified and technically proficient subcontractors.

  • Hydrotesting Equipment for Gas Cylinders
  • Gas User Manifolds and Filling Station Setups for various gases (Hydrogen, Oxygen, Argon, etc.)
  • Manifold Solutions for all gas types, including Skids and Banks
  • Comprehensive Gas Handling Equipment: Pumps, Valves, Regulators, Safety Relief Valves, and Cylinder Racks
  • Flexible Hose Assemblies, High-Pressure Pig Tails, and Custom Piping Solutions for all gases, including High Purity and Vacuum Jacketed Piping
  • CGA Fittings: We can make fittings suitable for Valves, Fittings, Flanges, Cylinder Valves, Regulators, etc.

We supply products that meet national and international standards, backed by a skilled workforce and responsive after-sales service to ensure top-quality performance.
